# Port Meridian

Port Meridian is an original top-down 2.5D open-world crime game built with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. It has a seeded procedural city with Harbour, Neon Row, Old Town, and Docks districts; arcade driving; four original stealable car models; traffic; wandering pedestrians; wanted levels and police pressure; combat; cash and health; three short story missions for Mara Voss; a live minimap; and procedural WebAudio.

## Controls
WASD or arrow keys move/drive. E enters or exits a car. F punches and raises heat. M mutes procedural audio. P pauses. Click or Enter starts a new game. Screenshot scenes use `?shot=start|drive|wanted|mission&seed=1`.

All characters, names, art, vehicles, story, sound, and music are original. The game uses no network requests, external libraries, downloaded assets, or build step. Licensed under MIT.

## Day 2
Police cars pursue the player at wanted levels 1–5, with sirens, minimap markers, collisions, and BUSTED/WASTED outcomes. Wanted heat decays by hiding stationary on foot inside a park for about 25–30 seconds at three stars; police sight pauses the decay. Mission 2 starts at three stars after the first delivery and advances when the heat reaches zero.
